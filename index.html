ScriptHelper

A WhatsApp and Telegram assistant that answers Zimbabwean pharmacists’ medicine questions, and refuses when it can’t answer safely.

Pharmacists need quick, reliable information about the medicines in front of them. The tools that could help either don’t live on the channels they actually use, or they confidently make things up. ScriptHelper is the answer to both problems.

Every clinical reply is grounded in a source it can cite, EDLIZ 2020 and the FDA’s product labelling, and if neither covers the question, it declines rather than guess. It’s decision-support, not clinical advice.

The one rule the whole system defends: a clinical answer only ever reaches a verified, licensed pharmacist, and the system never fabricates one.

How I think
(02)

A few things I don’t bend on.

01

Refuse before you guess.

Honesty is the feature, not the fallback. A clean “I don’t have that” beats a confident wrong answer every time, especially in medicine.

02

Fail loud.

An error should look like an error, never like an answer. The two can never be allowed to read the same to the person on the other end.

03

Urgency.

Moving fast and being careful aren’t opposites to me: I ship quickly because the safety checks are already built in. When something matters, I believe getting something out quick is important.

04

Judgment is the real work.

I build with AI tools heavily and I’m not shy about it. But what to refuse, what to cite, what counts as safe, that part is mine, and it’s the part that matters.
